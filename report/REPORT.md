## 1. Problem framing

**What "good" means for Uber_Support specifically:** unlike a retailer,
a wrong or overconfident reply here carries real safety and liability risk
(a driver-safety complaint mishandled by a bot is a much bigger problem than
a wrong reply to "does this come in blue"). So "good" for this agent is
defined as:
- High recall on escalating anything safety- or legal-risk adjacent, even
  at the cost of over-escalating some things a human would have auto-handled.
- Replies that never invent specific facts (amounts, policy details) not
  present in the conversation or in a grounding example.
- Replies that match the brand's actual historical resolution *pattern*
  (e.g., "ask for trip ID via DM"), not just plausible-sounding text.

**What I chose not to build:**
- No multi-turn dialogue management — the agent handles one incoming
  message with up to 2 turns of context, not an ongoing back-and-forth
  conversation state. (See decision log #2.)
- No actual account/refund actions — this drafts a *reply*, it doesn't call
  any real Uber system. Auto-handle means "send this reply automatically,"
  not "execute a refund."
- No embeddings-based retrieval — TF-IDF was sufficient at this scale and
  keeps the system dependency-light and debuggable (decision log #5).

  comes back after an auto-handled reply and the follow-up needs different
